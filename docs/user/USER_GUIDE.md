# ResultScope customer and staff guide

## Start a conversation

Open the homepage and choose Start a conversation, or open /app. Type your question in your preferred language. The website controls are English. Suggested questions are optional shortcuts. A report is not required. AI requires configured models; an unavailable connection is shown as an error, not a fabricated reply.

## Account and health checks

Use the account control to create a demonstration account or sign in. Passwords need at least 12 characters. Email verification and password recovery are not connected in this coursework build. Health checks shows the current packages and prices; ask the assistant to compare inclusions, budget and overlaps. Our centers opens a real map when configured; the locations are simulated service areas.

## Reports

Add a PNG, JPEG or one-to-three-page PDF under 3 MB, or select one of six synthetic samples. The OCR reads actual document content. Inspect the source image, correct each test, value, unit, reference and flag, and confirm the report belongs to the person being discussed. Only confirmed fields enter conversation context. The source preview currently displays the first PDF page; use the original PDF to verify later pages.

My reports allows selection of current and previous reports, field review and deletion. Compare only the same person, compatible units/methods and known collection dates. Delete report and history clears saved conversational copies of report values; deployment backups follow the operator's backup policy. Clearing the selected report alone does not delete the stored report.

## Appointments and payments

Ask for a package, branch, date and time. Review the assistant's preview before confirming. The server checks the current price and slot capacity again. My appointments shows booking and payment states separately. PromptPay/card uses test checkout; pay-at-center stays pending until staff records receipt. A success page or uploaded slip never proves payment. Rescheduling, cancellation and refund requests follow the published policy; time-sensitive and paid cases go to staff.

## People and organizations

Talk to our team requests staff help and shares the current conversation. The bot pauses during the queued/staff conversation. Staff sign in at /staff, take over a case, reply, prepare organization quotations and coordinate appointments. Managers can edit prices and review refunds. The dedicated clinical role currently has staff-class permissions; this is not a clinician-signoff workflow. New messages update by polling while the relevant tab is visible. Use Refresh inbox for an immediate queue refresh.

An organization quote is prepared by staff, reviewed by the customer and explicitly accepted. Employer coordination does not provide access to employee lab reports. LINE uses the same backend after an explicit one-use account-link invitation; unlink from the account control when needed. Do not share the linking URL.

## Limits

The assistant provides source-supported educational explanations and business coordination. It does not diagnose, prescribe or change treatment. Critical concerns need appropriate professional care and should not wait for a staff queue. Business records, branches and payment receipts in this coursework are simulations; medical sources remain real and attributed.
