# Decisions before a partner evaluation

These decisions turn the current explanation workspace into a defined product
pilot. They do not block the bounded UI/documentation refresh.

| Decision | Information needed | Resulting product work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First customer and operator | Named laboratory, clinic or pharmacy; actual user roles | Pilot scope, access and support ownership |
| Intended use | Education versus staff-assisted review; professional escalation | Claims, consent language and evaluation protocol |
| Approved sources | Organization identity, authorized source pack, release rights | Release-eligible corpus, contact details, policies and services |
| Primary user language | English, Thai or bilingual workflow; comprehension study | Interface localization and explanation evaluation |
| Input/report boundaries | Report formats, units, laboratories and allowed identifiers | OCR contract, correction flow and retention policy |
| Provider operation | Approved providers, model versions, limits and retention | Bounded live validation and operating costs |
| Distribution | Controlled local demo, private pilot or hosted product | Hosting, identity, cloud secrets and distributed quotas |
| Future integration | Named HIS/pharmacy partner and authorized data contract | Separate integration design; no FHIR/interface claim yet |
| Product identity and rights | Name/domain clearance and upstream code/data rights | Commercial catalog and distribution decision |

Use synthetic or de-identified examples until the data and access rules are approved.
Do not insert an invented business identity, service price or partner into the README.
