# Brand guide

## Name and promise

Use **ResultScope Laboratory Assistant** as the full public name. In a compact header, the mark may sit beside **ResultScope** with **Laboratory Assistant** on the second line. Use **ResultScope** in subsequent prose when context is clear. Repository names, Python identifiers, API paths, environment variables, and stable technical IDs retain their existing forms.

The product line is **Laboratory results, in context.** The promise is to help readers understand laboratory information while showing the limits of that understanding. This repository does not establish trademark clearance.

## Visual identity

The approved C + 2 direction uses primary purple `#4B0082`, ghost white `#F8F8FF`, violet `#8A2BE2`, lavender `#6A5ACD`, and secondary purple `#483D8B`. Text ink is `#21172F`. The owner permits diffuse gradients in hero and chat; solid white forms and answer surfaces preserve readability. Native desktop scrolling connects the landing to the conversation. White surfaces and subtle borders organize the document drawer and administration. Amber identifies supplied-range exceptions; restrained red identifies errors. Written labels carry meaning alongside color.

The implemented visual system is in [DESIGN.md](../../DESIGN.md) and `static/css/tokens.css`. Identity assets are `static/img/mark.svg`, `static/img/logo.svg`, and `static/img/favicon.svg`. Reuse them rather than inventing an unreviewed medical emblem or implying institutional affiliation.

IBM Plex Sans provides the English interface; Noto Sans Thai supplies Thai glyphs. Both are self-hosted under `static/fonts/`, with license files retained. Use sentence case and readable spacing. Do not return to spectral backgrounds, glow, decorative canvas, AI mascots, or science-fiction animation.

## Voice

Write as a careful guide: direct, calm, formal, and understandable. Lead with the action or observation. Distinguish supplied values, what the source says, and what remains unknown. A reference range is not a universal definition of health.

| Prefer | Avoid |
|---|---|
| “Use the range printed on your own report.” | “We know your normal range.” |
| “Review and edit every extracted field.” | “Perfectly accurate scanning.” |
| “No matching source was found.” | “The AI knows the answer.” |
| “This value is above the supplied range.” | “You have a disease.” |
| “Questions to take forward.” | “Your treatment plan.” |
| “Provider access is unavailable.” | Raw exceptions or provider responses |

Do not claim diagnostic accuracy, clinical validation, regulatory approval, HIPAA compliance, production readiness, customers, contracts, or measured benefits without a current record establishing the exact claim. “Source-linked” describes traceability; it does not mean every explanation is correct.

## Product communication

Show the actual application. Caption synthetic data and mocked responses close to the image. Mark future integrations as proposed, with dashed connectors when diagrams include them. Do not render future HIS or pharmacy capabilities as available menu items or customer integrations.

For executives, lead with the laboratory workflow and business hypotheses. Keep engineering detail in developer documentation. Make the educational boundary and readiness status easy to find without turning the interface into a wall of warnings.
