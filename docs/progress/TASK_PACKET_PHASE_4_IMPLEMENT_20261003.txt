   malformed response, timeout, cancellation, or store failure.

   reported as success.
5. User/provider/corpus text cannot execute as HTML or unsafe links; sensitive values are
   absent from logs and client-facing errors.

   live provider validation remains `NOT_RUN` under owner constraint.
