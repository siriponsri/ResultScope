# Product contract ResultScope 3

ResultScope is an LLM-led health-check business application. `/` presents the center, `/app` is the customer workspace, `/staff` is the staff workspace, and `/lab` retains the v2 educational workspace. The LLM chooses conversational intent, clarification, retrieval and proposed actions; there is no symbolic clinical intent router in the business route.

Included: individual and organization package discovery; quotations; confirmed appointment booking; sandbox payment or pay-at-center records; web staff chat; persistent account/history/report context; LINE adapter and worker; real-reference educational explanations. Background code owns identity, exact values, canonical prices, capacity, transaction transitions, schema validation and usage limits. A model cannot authorize its own action.

Business fixtures are simulation data. Medical explanations use the real public evidence catalogue only. Synthetic laboratory reports and their oracle never enter RAG. A report's confirmed units/ranges take precedence; missing means unknown. Reference intervals are not diagnostic decision limits. No diagnosis, prescribing, treatment changes, employer access to employee results, home collection or real-money charge is supported.

Current implementation evidence, live-test limits and course submission gates are in docs/business-v3/VERIFICATION.md. This source package is not a clinical release. The 2035 direction is a design scenario, not a forecast or claim of autonomous care.
