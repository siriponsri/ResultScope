A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before we talk.

Can I ask in Thai or another language?

Yes. The assistant follows the language of your conversation and asks for clarification when needed. The website interface is in English.

Can the assistant diagnose a condition?

No. It explains supported information and helps you prepare questions. Medical assessment and treatment decisions belong with an appropriate professional.

Can I speak with a person?

Yes. Ask in the chat or use the staff contact option. Outside service hours, your request is queued without a promise of immediate response.

How are bookings and payments handled?

You review the details before confirming a booking. This coursework environment uses test payments and pay-at-center records; it does not take real payments.
