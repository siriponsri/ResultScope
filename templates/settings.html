What happens to a message?

The safety model checks the request. The conversation model chooses a response strategy and search query. ResultScope retrieves verified source records, the model drafts its answer, and source/value checks plus an output safety check run before the answer appears.

A normal turn uses five model calls, including a separate evidence review. Optional semantic retrieval adds a service request. Report reading is separate. There is no automatic paid fallback or hidden retry.
