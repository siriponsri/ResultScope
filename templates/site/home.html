Questions people ask first

Can I ask in Thai or another language?

Yes. The assistant replies in the language you write in. The website itself is in English.

Who answers, the AI or a person?

Two AI roles answer straight away: the Health-check Advisor and the Report Explainer. Each message shows which one answered. A person on our team confirms appointments and quotations, and takes over whenever you ask.

Can the assistant diagnose a condition?

No. It explains what tests measure and how your confirmed values compare with the range printed on your report, with sources. Diagnosis and treatment belong with a clinician.

Is my appointment booked straight away?

Your request holds the slot. A team member confirms or declines it and you are notified either way. {{ policies.cancellation_policy }}

How do payments work here?

This is a simulation. Pay at the center, or use a test PromptPay or card payment that moves no real money. {{ policies.payment_policy }}
