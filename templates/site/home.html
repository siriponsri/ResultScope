Questions people ask first

More detail on Help and policies.

Who answers my questions, an AI or a person?

Two AI roles answer straight away: the Health-check Advisor for packages and booking, and the Report Explainer for your report. Each answer shows which one replied. A person on our team confirms appointments and quotations, and takes over whenever you ask.

Can I ask in Thai?

Yes. Answers follow the language you write in. The website itself is in English.

Is this a real clinic?

No. ResultScope is a coursework business simulation. Packages, prices and centers are simulated and no clinical service takes place.

Is my appointment booked straight away?

Your request holds the slot until a team member confirms or declines it. {{ policies.cancellation_policy }}

How do payments work here?

{{ policies.payment_policy }}

Can it tell me whether I am ill?

No. It explains what a test measures and how your confirmed value compares with the range printed on your report, with sources. Diagnosis and treatment belong with a clinician.

Who can see my report?

Only you. You check every value that was read before it is used, and deleting a report also clears conversation history that could contain its values.
