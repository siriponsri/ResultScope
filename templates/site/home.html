Common questions

Can I ask in Thai or another language?

Yes. The assistant replies in the language you write in. The website itself is in English.

Can the assistant diagnose a condition?

No. It explains what tests measure and what your confirmed values show against the printed range, with sources. Decisions about diagnosis and treatment belong with a clinician.

Is my appointment booked straight away?

Your request holds the slot. A team member confirms or declines it, and you are notified either way. {{ policies.cancellation_policy }}

How do payments work here?

This is a simulation. You can choose pay at the center, or a test PromptPay/card payment that moves no real money. {{ policies.payment_policy }}

When can I talk to a person?

Any time you ask in the chat or use “Talk to our team”. {{ policies.handoff_policy }}
