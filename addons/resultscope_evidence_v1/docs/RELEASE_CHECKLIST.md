## Must be checked against the actual integrated local candidate

- [ ] 4A public-reference route mounted without changing business/synthetic boundaries
- [ ] 4B evidence reaches real provider abstraction and validated JSON/SSE consistently
- [ ] First-screen main upload/question flow; OCR correction; usable failure/reset states

- [ ] Live Typhoon smoke test with owner key, allowed data, actual model ID and latency
- [ ] Live guard/model evidence if claimed by the product; Clef optional and independently gated
- [ ] Actual user comprehension observed; screenshots alone are insufficient

## Coursework evidence (teacher materials supplied with the task)

- [ ] Selected real business identity, purpose, owner-approved facts and contacts
- [ ] KB quantity/scope meeting rubric: at least 5 pages OR 15 business services; public reference PDFs alone do not prove real-business catalog completion
- [ ] Thai RAG answers with traceable sources, refusals and unsupported-query behavior
- [ ] 10 question cases, 5 image cases, 5 safety cases, with actual output, expected criteria, status and elapsed time
- [ ] Explicit live / mocked / retrieval-only labels and no fabricated PASS
- [ ] Three justified improvements with real before/after evidence
- [ ] Architecture, message flow, prompts/configuration, reproducible source/setup
- [ ] Report in requested Google Docs/PDF submission format; demo video script and actual recording
- [ ] Submission link/access checked by owner; no automated submission is performed here

The supplied Week 12 handout has both a 3-minute overview and a later 4-minute video statement. Prepare a ≤3-minute demo to satisfy the stricter limit, unless the teacher clarifies otherwise. The handout lists Oct 3 business selection, Oct 10 progress/exam, Oct 17 submission and Oct 24 presentation; confirm these course-specific dates with the teacher if they have changed.

## Before deployment with real use

- [ ] Source use/redistribution permissions and clinical content ownership/review resolved
- [ ] Intended-use wording, business release approval and patient safety boundaries reviewed
- [ ] Target hosting architecture/auth/tenant boundary/data retention settled
- [ ] Secret management, rate/resource limits, file handling, sanitized logs and monitoring checked
- [ ] Persistence, backup, rollback and deletion behavior tested where applicable
- [ ] Actual partner sandbox and authorization before HIS/pharmacy connection
- [ ] Explicit owner deployment instruction after reviewing the local candidate

Public-reference demo readiness, coursework completeness, business release readiness and clinical/production readiness are separate decisions.
