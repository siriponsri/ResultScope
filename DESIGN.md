# ResultScope design — conversation first

Retain the original purple identity: paper `#F8F8FF`, surface `#FFFFFF`, ink `#21172F`,
muted `#61586F`, primary `#4B0082`, hover `#390064`, accent `#8A2BE2`, lavender
`#6A5ACD`, tint `#F1EBFC`. IBM Plex Sans and Noto Sans Thai are self-hosted.

Use generous space, restrained borders, solid reading surfaces, modest radii and clear
hierarchy. The composer is the primary control. Suggested questions are optional
shortcuts; no upload wizard precedes chat. Source chips open original references.
The report dialog supports pixel inspection, field correction and explicit confirmation.

Desktop: persistent left navigation, quiet top bar and centered conversation. Mobile:
hidden navigation drawer, full-width composer, native dialogs and stacked report review.
Preserve native scrolling, keyboard focus, text zoom, real error states and reduced motion.
Decorative document artwork contains no invented laboratory values.

GSAP animates entry and individual messages. Hyperframes 0.8.131 supplies the actual
12-second introduction composition/player. It is optional, user-controlled, silent and
finite. Reduced motion skips entry animation; the introduction opens on its held frame.
Never use animations as fake model progress or reveal unguarded answer tokens.
